What Chemarket optimizes for

Verified partners only

Onboard only vetted chemical buyers and suppliers. Company registration and basic checks are completed before they appear in your workspace, so you avoid generic directory noise.

📄

Structured RFQs, not loose emails

RFQs capture specs, volume, packaging, delivery window and documentation requirements up front, so suppliers can respond once instead of asking the same questions over and over.

🛡️

Compliance and audit trail

Every RFQ, quote, message and document is stored with the decision. That makes it easier to support REACH, TSCA, GMP and ISO-driven audits or customer questions months later.
